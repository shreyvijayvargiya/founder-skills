# SEO Opportunity Operator

## Mission

Turn a SaaS website and its market into a prioritized SEO opportunity map.

Do not dump thousands of keywords.

Find the opportunities most likely to matter to the business.

## Input

Required:

- SaaS website URL

Optional:

- discovery report
- target country
- target audience
- competitor URLs
- existing keyword data

If a discovery report exists, use it instead of repeating unnecessary research.

## Phase 1 — Existing SEO Inventory

Inspect the website and identify important existing pages.

Categorize:

- homepage
- product
- features
- pricing
- use cases
- industries
- integrations
- alternatives
- comparisons
- blog
- guides
- resources
- templates/tools

For each important page determine:

- purpose
- apparent search intent
- target topic
- quality
- optimization opportunity

## Phase 2 — Search Universe

Research opportunities across:

### Category

What is the product category?

### Problem

What problems are people trying to solve?

### Use case

What jobs are people trying to accomplish?

### Audience

Who searches for this solution?

### Industry

Which industries have legitimate use cases?

### Features

What capabilities are searched for independently?

### Alternatives

What competitor alternatives are searched?

### Comparisons

What products are compared?

### Integrations

What integrations may create useful search demand?

### Educational

What questions does the target audience ask?

### Templates / Tools

Would a template, calculator, generator or free tool be useful?

## Phase 3 — Customer Language Research

Use available web/search capabilities to investigate how real users describe
the problem.

When useful, research sources such as:

- Reddit
- G2
- Capterra
- forums
- communities
- search results
- competitor content

Generate search queries dynamically.

Do not blindly use fixed queries.

## Phase 4 — Competitor Gap

Inspect relevant competitor websites.

Find:

- pages they have that the SaaS lacks
- topics they cover
- alternative pages
- comparison pages
- use cases
- industry pages
- integration pages
- content clusters

Do not copy competitor content.

Use competitors to discover demand and page structures.

## Phase 5 — Opportunity Types

Identify:

1. Commercial category pages
2. Alternative pages
3. Comparison pages
4. Use-case pages
5. Industry pages
6. Audience pages
7. Integration pages
8. Educational content
9. Templates
10. Free tools
11. Programmatic opportunities

Only recommend pages with a meaningful unique purpose.

## Phase 6 — Prioritization

Score important opportunities using:

### Business Value

1–5

### Product Relevance

1–5

### Search Opportunity

1–5

### Competition

1–5, where 5 means relatively favorable

### Strategic Value

1–5

Use the total only as a prioritization aid.

Do not present it as an official SEO metric.

## Reference — Keyword & Intent Frameworks

Use these when researching and prioritizing. Adapt to the SaaS — do not dump generic keyword lists.

### Search Intent Types

Match every recommended page to intent or it will not rank:

| Intent | User goal | Content type | SaaS example |
|--------|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Informational | Learn something | Blog, guide | "how to manage client projects" |
| Navigational | Find a specific site | Homepage, brand | "[product name] login" |
| Commercial | Research before buying | Comparison, review | "best project management for agencies" |
| Transactional | Sign up or buy | Product, pricing, signup | "project management software free trial" |

### Keyword Research Process

1. **Seed keywords** — What does the product sell? What problems does it solve? What do customers call the category?
2. **Expand** — Use search results, Reddit, G2, forums, competitor pages, Google autocomplete, People Also Ask, related searches.
3. **Evaluate** — Volume (enough to matter), difficulty (can this site realistically rank?), intent (matches the product?), business value (will traffic convert?).

### Keyword Difficulty (Quick Check)

Google the target keyword. Review top 3 results: domain strength, content depth, backlinks to page. Ask: can this SaaS create something better and more relevant?

| Tier | What you are up against | When to target |
|------|-------------------------|----------------|
| Low | Thin content, weak domains | Start here — long-tail, niche use cases |
| Medium | Mixed quality, some authority | After early wins |
| High | Established sites, strong content | With growing authority |
| Very high | Major brands | Long-term — avoid as first targets |

Prefer long-tail and niche terms early: lower competition, higher intent, faster wins.

### Long-Tail Discovery

| Method | How |
|--------|-----|
| Google Autocomplete | Type seed keyword, note suggestions |
| People Also Ask | Expand PAA boxes in SERPs |
| Related searches | Bottom of Google results |
| Reddit / Quora / forums | How real users phrase problems |
| Competitor pages | Topics and headings they cover |

### Keyword Mapping

Map one primary keyword per page. Include in output when useful:

| Page | Primary keyword | Secondary keywords | Intent |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|--------|
| Homepage | brand + category | variations | Navigational |
| Product | product type | features, use cases | Commercial / Transactional |
| Blog / guide | how-to topic | related questions | Informational |

Do not create separate pages when intent is substantially identical.

### E-E-A-T Lens

When scoring opportunities, favor topics where the SaaS can demonstrate:

| Signal | How |
|--------|-----|
| Experience | First-hand product use, original insights |
| Expertise | Depth, accuracy, author/product credibility |
| Authoritativeness | Citations, brand mentions, strong pages |
| Trustworthiness | HTTPS, clear ownership, accurate claims |

### Keyword Mistakes to Avoid

- Targeting impossible head terms ("CRM") before niche wins ("CRM for small agencies")
- Ignoring search intent (product page for informational query)
- Recommending thin pages for topics that need depth
- Duplicate or near-duplicate pages for the same intent
