# Content Decay Recovery

## Mission

Recover value from content that has lost traffic, rankings, or relevance.

Use when the user asks:

- Which content should I update?
- Why did my traffic decline?
- Which old articles are losing rankings?
- What content should I refresh?
- How can I recover organic traffic?

The goal is not to update everything.

The goal is to recover the highest-value declining pages first.

---

# Input

## Required

- Website URL

## Optional

- Search Console data
- Analytics data
- Ranking data
- Publication dates
- Target keywords
- Competitor URLs

---

# Workflow

## 1. Find Decaying Content

Research:

- Old articles
- Ranking changes
- Traffic changes
- Search visibility
- Publication dates
- Current relevance

Prioritize pages showing meaningful decline.

---

## 2. Diagnose The Decay

Determine possible causes:

- Outdated information
- Search intent changed
- Competitors improved
- Missing topics
- Weak internal links
- Poor SERP coverage
- Outdated entities
- Thin sections

Separate evidence from assumptions.

---

## 3. Recovery Strategy

Recommend:

- Content refresh
- New sections
- Query expansion
- Intent alignment
- Entity updates
- Internal links
- Consolidation
- Redirects when appropriate

### Reference — Historical Optimization

Content-led companies (HubSpot, Ahrefs) recover traffic by **updating winners**, not only publishing new posts.

| Action | When |
|--------|------|
| Refresh stats and examples | Outdated data is the decay signal |
| Expand thin sections | Competitors now cover subtopics you miss |
| Realign to current intent | SERP format changed (e.g. listicle → guide) |
| Add internal links | Page became orphaned or cluster grew |
| Consolidate duplicates | Two pages compete for same intent |
| Republish and redistribute | Major refresh — treat like a new launch |

After a major update, repurpose for distribution (summary thread, newsletter, LinkedIn post).

Do not rewrite pages with no decay signal or business value.

---

## 4. Prioritize

Score opportunities by:

- Traffic potential
- Business value
- Ranking opportunity
- Content effort
- Freshness

# Quality Rules

- Do not claim traffic decline without evidence.
- Do not invent Search Console or Analytics data.
- Separate observed signals from assumptions.
- Do not recommend unnecessary rewrites.
- Prioritize high-impact recovery opportunities.
