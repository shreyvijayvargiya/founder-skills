# SEO Content Planner

## Mission

Turn SEO opportunities into a practical publishing system.

The goal is not to create the largest possible content calendar.

The goal is to create the smallest set of pages that can create meaningful
search coverage and business value.

## Input

Accept:

- SEO Opportunity Report
- SaaS website URL
- keyword list
- competitor research

If only a URL is provided, perform enough discovery to create the plan.

## Phase 1 — Group Opportunities

Cluster opportunities into topics.

Example:

```text
CRM
├── CRM software
├── CRM for startups
├── CRM for agencies
├── CRM alternatives
├── CRM comparisons
└── CRM guides

Avoid creating separate pages when search intent is substantially identical.

Phase 2 — Build Page Architecture

For each cluster identify:





pillar page



commercial pages



supporting pages



comparison pages



alternative pages



use-case pages



educational pages

Phase 3 — Prioritize

Use:





business value



search intent



product relevance

strategic importance

Prioritize commercial and high-intent pages when appropriate.

Do not automatically prioritize informational content.

Target audience

Recommended title

H1

Phase 5 — Internal Linking

Build a logical relationship between:

Phase 6 — Publishing Plan

Create:

## Reference — Startup SEO Priorities

Focus in this order when building the plan:

1. **Technical foundation** — Crawlable, fast, mobile-friendly (route to launch-foundation-audit if live site gaps exist)
2. **Keyword strategy** — Know what pages target which intents
3. **On-page basics** — Titles, H1s, metadata on existing pages
4. **Content creation** — Quality over quantity; commercial pages before pure blog volume
5. **Internal linking** — Connect new and existing pages (pillar-cluster model)
6. **Link building** — After content worth linking to exists; directories and linkable assets first for startups

### Low-Budget Tactics to Include

| Tactic | Cost | Impact |
|--------|------|--------|
| Optimize existing pages | Free | High |
| Google Search Console | Free | High |
| Answer Quora / Reddit with genuine value | Free | Medium |
| HARO / journalist requests | Free | Medium |
| One exceptional pillar or tool | Time only | High |
| Fix technical issues | Free / cheap | High |
| Internal linking pass | Free | Medium |

### Content That Earns Links

When planning Month 2+, consider one linkable asset per quarter:

| Type | Example | Why it gets links |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| Original research | "State of [industry] report" | Unique data to cite |
| Comprehensive guide | "Complete guide to X" | Best resource on topic |
| Free tool | Calculator, generator, template | Utility value |
| Expert roundup | "How [N] experts do X" | Participants share |

Do not plan link building before publishable content exists.

### 90-Day Plan Template

Extend the 30-day plan when the founder has capacity. Adapt weeks to the SaaS:

**Month 1 — Foundation**

| Week | Tasks |
|------|-------|
| 1 | Keyword/opportunity map, competitive review |
| 2 | Technical audit, fix critical issues |
| 3 | On-page optimization of existing pages |
| 4 | Content plan, publish first pillar page |

**Month 2 — Content**

| Week | Tasks |
|------|-------|
| 5–6 | Publish 4 cluster posts linked to pillar |
| 7 | Create one linkable asset (research, tool, guide) |
| 8 | Internal linking audit and optimization |

**Month 3 — Growth**

| Week | Tasks |
|------|-------|
| 9–10 | Publish 4 more posts, begin outreach (directories, guest posts) |
| 11 | Guest post or podcast pitches (10–20) |
| 12 | Review GSC/GA4 metrics, plan next quarter |

### Planning Mistakes to Avoid

- Targeting impossible keywords before long-tail wins
- Publishing volume without intent alignment
- Orphan pages with no internal links
- No measurement plan (GSC + GA4 from day one)

## Reference — Content Strategy Frameworks

Use when building clusters, briefs, and the publishing plan.

### Pillar-Cluster Model

```
Pillar page (comprehensive, 3,000–5,000 words)
├── Cluster post 1 (specific subtopic, 1,500–2,500 words)
├── Cluster post 2
├── Cluster post 3
└── All interlinked to pillar and each other
```

Why it works: signals topic authority, creates internal linking structure, captures long-tail keywords, guides the reader journey.

When planning clusters, name the pillar first, then 4–6 cluster topics that each deserve their own page.

### Content Moat Types

Prioritize gaps and pillars where the SaaS can build defensible advantage:

| Type | Description | Defensibility |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| Data moat | Proprietary data only you have | Very high |
| Expertise moat | Deep domain knowledge | High |
| Volume moat | Comprehensive coverage of a topic | Medium |
| Community moat | User-generated content, templates | Very high |
| Brand moat | Trusted name drives clicks | High |

Ask: what can only this SaaS create? Invest disproportionately there.

### 70-20-10 Content Mix

| Share | Type | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| 70% | Core content | SEO-driven pillar/cluster, proven topics |
| 20% | Thought leadership | Original POV, trends, industry commentary |
| 10% | Experimental | New formats, bold angles |

Apply when sequencing the publishing plan — do not make everything experimental or everything SEO-only.

### Jobs-to-be-Done Content Map

Map planned pages to customer jobs:

| Job | Content type | Example |
|-----|--------------|---------|
| Learn | Educational | "What is [topic]?" guides |
| Compare | Comparison | "[Tool A] vs [Tool B]" |
| Do | How-to | "How to [accomplish goal]" |
| Validate | Social proof | Case studies, testimonials |
| Buy | Bottom-funnel | Pricing, demos, trials |

Ensure the plan covers the journey — not only top-of-funnel blog posts.

### Pillar Selection Criteria

Score each proposed pillar 1–5:

| Criteria | Question |
|----------|----------|
| Search volume | Do people search for this? |
| Business relevance | Does it connect to the product? |
| Expertise | Can we create authoritative content? |
| Competition | Can we realistically rank? |
| Evergreen | Will this matter in 2 years? |

Prefer 2–4 pillars deep over many shallow topics.

### Content Velocity by Stage

| Stage | Blog posts/month | Other |
|-------|------------------|-------|
| Early (founder only) | 2–4 | 1 template, consistent social |
| Growth (1 marketer) | 4–8 | 2 templates, newsletter, video |
| Scale (team) | 8–16 | Full content mix |

Right-size the 30/90-day plan to team capacity — do not plan 16 posts for a solo founder.

### Editorial Workflow

| Stage | Time | Checklist |
|-------|------|-----------|
| Ideation | Ongoing | Keywords, audience questions, competitor gaps |
| Brief | 30 min | Keyword, outline, angle, CTA |
| Draft | 2–4 hours | First draft from brief |
| Edit | 1–2 hours | Clarity, accuracy, voice |
| SEO review | 30 min | Keywords, meta, internal links |
| Publish | 30 min | CMS, final check |
| Distribute | 2 hours | All channels (see saas-launch distribution reference) |

Every published piece needs a distribution plan — not publish-only.

### Content Metrics by Goal

| Goal | Primary metrics | Secondary metrics |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Awareness | Pageviews, unique visitors, social reach | Time on page, bounce rate |
| Engagement | Comments, shares, email signups | Scroll depth, return visits |
| SEO | Rankings, organic traffic, backlinks | Indexed pages, average position |
| Conversion | Signups, MQLs, pipeline influenced | Assisted conversions |

Blog benchmarks (directional): 2+ min session duration good, 3+ great; organic traffic share 30%+ good, 50%+ great.

Content ROI (simple): `(Revenue attributed to content − Content cost) / Content cost`. Track content touchpoints in the customer journey when possible.

### Content-Led Patterns (Apply, Don't Copy)

- **Ahrefs-style:** Dogfood the product in content; update old posts; original data where possible; aim to be the best page on the topic.
- **HubSpot-style:** Pillar-cluster depth; spin educational content into free tools; historical optimization of winners.
- **Notion-style:** Templates and user-created assets as distribution; make content easy to share.
- **Buffer-style:** Consistent publishing; unique angle (transparency, POV) others won't replicate.

### Content Strategy Mistakes to Avoid

1. Publishing without a distribution plan
2. Writing what you want, not what people search for
3. Inconsistent publishing — strong start then silence
4. Never updating old posts (route decaying pages to content-decay-recovery)
5. Vanity metrics (pageviews) without conversion path
6. Too many pillars — spread thin instead of going deep
7. Not checking what already ranks before briefing
8. No internal linking between cluster pieces
9. Gating everything before providing value
10. One-and-done — not repurposing for distribution
