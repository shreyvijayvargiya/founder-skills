# Cold Outreach Writer

## Mission

Convert **where to find leads** into **what to say** — short, respectful sequences founders can send.

Use when the user asks:

- Write cold emails for my ICP
- DM sequence for communities you found
- Outreach copy after lead research
- First touch messages for my SaaS

Requires context from **lead-research-intelligence** or equivalent (ICP, sources, angles).

Do not recommend purchased lists, scraping inboxes, or spam tactics.

---

# Input

## Required

- Product and ICP (role, company size, problem)
- Outreach channel (email, LinkedIn DM, X DM, community DM)

## Optional

- lead-research-intelligence output (sources, communities, angles)
- customer-voice-mining phrases
- saas-discovery positioning
- Proof points (case study, demo, free tool — only if real)
- Sender persona (founder vs sales)
- Sequence length (default: 3 touches)
- Geography / compliance notes (GDPR, CAN-SPAM — remind, don't legal-advise)

---

# Workflow

## 1. Define The Wedge

Choose one primary wedge per sequence:

- Specific pain in customer language
- Trigger event (hiring, funding, tool switch — only if user provided signal)
- Outcome metric the ICP cares about
- Peer proof (industry, role) without fake logos

One clear CTA per message:

- Reply, book demo, try free, watch 2-min demo

---

## 2. Personalization Slots

Every template must include explicit placeholders:

- `{first_name}`
- `{company}`
- `{specific_observation}` (from their site, post, or job — user fills)
- `{pain_hypothesis}`

Explain what research the sender should do in 2 minutes before sending.

---

## 3. Write The Sequence

### Touch 1 — Problem + relevance

- Subject line options (2) for email
- Body ≤120 words for email; shorter for DM
- Single ask

### Touch 2 — Value + proof angle

- Bump thread; new insight or micro-case
- No guilt-trip "just circling back"

### Touch 3 — Breakup or alternate angle

- Polite close or different wedge
- Offer resource (demo, checklist) if real

For LinkedIn/X DMs: even shorter; no attachments pitch in touch 1.

---

## 4. Channel Variants

If multiple channels requested, adapt — do not duplicate the same text.

Note platform limits (LinkedIn connection note length, etc.) at high level.

# Quality Rules

- No deceptive subject lines or fake "Re:" threads.
- No guaranteed reply rates.
- Respect opt-out and platform anti-spam rules.
- Founder-authentic tone; avoid bulk-sales clichés.
- Do not invent mutual connections or fake personalization.
