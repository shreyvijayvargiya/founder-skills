# Founder Decision Framework

Founder OS should not stop at research.

Research exists to improve decisions.

Use this framework when the founder is deciding whether to build, change, launch, or continue pursuing an opportunity.

---

# BUILD

Recommend BUILD when:

* The problem is sufficiently validated.
* The target customer is reasonably clear.
* There is evidence of demand.
* Existing alternatives leave a meaningful opportunity.
* Monetization appears plausible.
* The founder has a realistic path to reach customers.
* Remaining uncertainty can be handled during development.

BUILD does not mean certainty.

It means the evidence is strong enough to justify building.

---

# MODIFY

Recommend MODIFY when:

* The underlying problem appears real.
* The current product idea is poorly positioned.
* The target customer should change.
* The feature set is too broad.
* Differentiation is weak.
* Another use case appears significantly stronger.

Example:

```text
Original:
CRM for every small business

Better:
WhatsApp lead management for service businesses
```

---

# TEST

Recommend TEST when:

* The problem appears promising.
* Important assumptions remain unvalidated.
* A cheap experiment can produce meaningful evidence.
* Building the full product would be premature.

Preferred tests:

* Customer interviews
* Landing page
* Waitlist
* Pre-order
* Concierge MVP
* Manual service
* Pricing test
* Outreach experiment
* Small paid campaign
* Prototype test

Prefer tests that produce behavioral evidence over opinions.

---

# WAIT

Recommend WAIT when:

* The opportunity is interesting but evidence is insufficient.
* Timing is uncertain.
* Market conditions are unclear.
* The founder lacks critical resources.
* A better opportunity currently exists.

WAIT should include what evidence would justify revisiting the opportunity.

---

# KILL

Recommend KILL when evidence strongly suggests:

* Weak or nonexistent demand
* Customers do not care enough to act
* Economics are structurally poor
* Acquisition is unrealistic
* The market is inaccessible
* Differentiation is not achievable
* Stronger alternatives dominate with little room for entry

KILL the current idea, not necessarily the underlying problem.

A killed product can lead to a better product.

---

# Decision Confidence

Use:

```text
HIGH
MEDIUM
LOW
```

Never express false precision.

Confidence should reflect evidence quality, not how confidently the answer is written.

---

# Decision Format

When appropriate:

```text
Decision: TEST
Confidence: Medium

Why:
...

Evidence:
...

What remains unknown:
...

Next experiment:
...

Success criteria:
...

Decision after test:
BUILD if ...
MODIFY if ...
KILL if ...
```

---

# Decision Priority

Prefer decisions that:

1. Reduce major uncertainty
2. Require low cost
3. Produce behavioral evidence
4. Can be executed quickly
5. Change the next decision

The goal is not perfect knowledge.

The goal is **better decisions with less wasted effort**.
