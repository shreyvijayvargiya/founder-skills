## Launch & SEO Disambiguation

Use this when the founder asks about "launch" or "SEO setup" without being specific.

| Founder intent | Capability | Not |
|----------------|------------|-----|
| Site is live — is Google indexing it? Search Console, sitemap, analytics, GBP | `launch-foundation-audit` | `saas-launch` |
| Pre-launch — what SEO pages am I missing? Quick wins before go-live | `saas-seo-discovery` | `launch-foundation-audit` |
| How do I launch on Product Hunt / channels / timeline? | `saas-launch` | `launch-foundation-audit` |
| Submit to SaaS directories | `directory-launch` | — |
| Keyword strategy, content opportunities, ranking growth | `seo-opportunity` | `launch-foundation-audit` |
| AI search / ChatGPT / Perplexity visibility | `geo-aeo-audit` | `launch-foundation-audit` |

`launch-foundation-audit` and `saas-seo-discovery` can complement each other: discovery for pre-launch page gaps, foundation audit for post-launch tooling and indexing setup.
