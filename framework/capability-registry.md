# Capability Registry

Read each capability's full instructions at `capabilities/<stage>/<name>/AGENT.md`.

Select the minimum capabilities required for the objective.

---

## Validate

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `saas-discovery` | Research a SaaS URL: product, ICP, competitors, and positioning. Use before SEO, content, competitor, or growth research. |
| `market-intelligence` | Analyze a market or category: competitors, demand, trends, risks, and opportunities. Use for market entry or positioning decisions. |
| `competitor-intelligence` | Research competitors: positioning, sentiment, strengths, weaknesses, and strategic moves. Use for competitive strategy. |
| `pricing-intelligence` | Research SaaS pricing, packaging, plans, and competitor pricing. Use to find pricing and packaging opportunities. |

---

## Build

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `saas-seo-discovery` | Baseline SEO audit before launch — site inventory, quick wins, and page gaps from your product URL. Use pre-launch or early site. |
| `micro-saas-builder` | Scope a micro-SaaS MVP: ICP, features, pricing, distribution, and launch plan. Use when validating a small product idea. |
| `lead-research-intelligence` | Find lead sources, communities, and outreach angles for your ICP. Use to build a customer acquisition pipeline. |
| `ai-agent-opportunity` | Find AI agent and automation opportunities from workflows, bottlenecks, and pain points. Use when exploring agent products. |

---

## Launch

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `launch-foundation-audit` | Audit post-launch website readiness — Search Console, indexing, sitemap, analytics, Google Business Profile, technical SEO, and business listings. Use when a site is live and needs foundational setup. |
| `saas-launch` | Build a SaaS launch plan: Product Hunt, channels, assets, and timeline. Use when planning a product launch. |
| `directory-launch` | Find quality SaaS directories and create a submission strategy with reusable listing copy. Use for directory launch. |

## Grow (SEO)

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `seo-opportunity` | Find and prioritize SaaS SEO pages, keywords, gaps, and commercial opportunities. Use for SEO strategy and page planning. |
| `seo-content-planner` | Turn SEO research into clusters, briefs, internal links, and a publishing roadmap. Use after SEO opportunity research. |
| `seo-page-generator` | Write a high-quality SaaS SEO page from a brief or topic. Use when ready to create title, meta, article, FAQ, and CTA. |
| `seo-authority-architect` | Design SEO site architecture, authority flow, internal linking, reverse silos, and entity relationships. Use to strengthen topical authority and important pages. |
| `internal-link-optimizer` | Improve SaaS internal linking, fix orphan pages, and connect SEO pages. Use for site structure and link optimization. |
| `content-gap` | Find content topics and formats competitors cover that your site is missing. Use after discovery or with a URL and competitor list. |
| `content-decay-recovery` | Identify declining content, diagnose ranking and traffic decay, and create recovery plans through updates, consolidation, and optimization. |
| `competitor-seo-page` | Plan SEO pages around a competitor: alternatives, comparisons, switching, and migration. Use when targeting a rival in search. |
| `query-fan-out-optimizer` | Expand a target topic into related queries, search intents, follow-up questions, entities, and answer opportunities for SEO, GEO, and AEO. |
| `geo-aeo-audit` | Audit AI search visibility for a SaaS in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity. Use for GEO/AEO and answer-engine readiness. |

## Launch & SEO Disambiguation

Use this when the founder asks about "launch" or "SEO setup" without being specific.

| Founder intent | Capability | Not |
|----------------|------------|-----|
| Site is live — is Google indexing it? Search Console, sitemap, analytics, GBP | `launch-foundation-audit` | `saas-launch` |
| Pre-launch — what SEO pages am I missing? Quick wins before go-live | `saas-seo-discovery` | `launch-foundation-audit` |
| How do I launch on Product Hunt / channels / timeline? | `saas-launch` | `launch-foundation-audit` |
| Submit to SaaS directories | `directory-launch` | — |
| Keyword strategy, content opportunities, ranking growth | `seo-opportunity` | `launch-foundation-audit` |
| AI search / ChatGPT / Perplexity visibility | `geo-aeo-audit` | `launch-foundation-audit` |

`launch-foundation-audit` and `saas-seo-discovery` can complement each other: discovery for pre-launch page gaps, foundation audit for post-launch tooling and indexing setup.
